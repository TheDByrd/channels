Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy covers "subtle-relaxing-upload", a private tool the owner of the Rainy Window Radio and Subtle Relaxing White Noise YouTube channels uses to manage those channels.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app asks for permission to upload and manage videos on the owner's YouTube channel and to read that channel's YouTube Analytics reports.

How the data is used

  • Uploading, scheduling and updating the owner's own videos and playlists.
  • Reading view and watch-time numbers for the owner's own videos to plan future content.

Storage and sharing

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. The app runs on that computer and has no server. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app does not access any other person's account or data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: through the contact details on the Rainy Window Radio channel page.
